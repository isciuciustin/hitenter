No landlord in the middle

// what it does

Everything a group needs

  • Communities and channels. Text and voice channels, roles, moderation. Nobody runs the community but its members.
  • DMs, end to end. Encrypted so only the two of you can read them.
  • Files. Sent straight between members' computers, only to people allowed to see them.
  • Voice. Group calls of up to eight, and calls from a DM.
  • Zaps. Tip a message in sats over Lightning.

// said plainly

No fine print

  • Group messages are not encrypted at rest. Every member's app keeps a copy, and the app says who can read a group.
  • Members can see your IP address when you connect to them directly, as in any peer-to-peer app.
  • Your key is yours. It stays in your system's keychain. Lose it without a backup and nobody can recover it, us included.
  • Zaps carry a 1% fee, paid as a separate payment on top and shown before you send. Zaps under 100 sats carry none.
  • No telemetry. The app reports nothing to us.
  • Open source, MIT licence. Read every line of it on GitHub. Third-party licences ship with every download.
